# 🚀 Adventure Works Cycles – Power BI

**Overview**  
Developed an end-to-end Power BI solution by importing data from SQL and applying Power Query for cleaning, relational modeling, and DAX for calculated metrics. Built interactive dashboards to analyze sales, costs, and profitability across markets. Key insights included the U.S. as the top market (followed by Australia) and consistent year-over-year growth. Delivered best practices for future analysis, emphasizing product categorization and evaluating discount impact on purchase behavior.

**Key insights**  
- U.S. is the largest customer base; Australia ranks second.  
- Northwestern Canada shows adoption adjacent to U.S. markets.  
- YoY cumulative revenue outperforms the current-period baseline, indicating sustained growth.  
- Future analysis: product categorization and discount influence (via `FactInternetSales`).
